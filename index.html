The Four Pillars of Aurelia

True luxury lies in the uncompromising pursuit of precision. Every harvest that graces our bar has undergone a relentless journey of curation, sensory calibration, and artisanal stewardship.

01

High Altitude Terroirs

Direct-Trade Single Origins

Sourced exclusively from shade-grown, family-owned micro-lots perched above 1,800 meters in Panama, Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

Harvest: Hand-Selected Cherries
02

Cast-Iron Micro Roasting

Bespoke Thermal Profiles

Roasted in small 5kg batches inside our vintage cast-iron drum. We honor the unique cellular density of each cultivar to unleash sublime floral and caramel notes.

Method: Conduction & Radiant Heat

Coffee is 98% water. Our reverse-osmosis filtration system re-introduces pure volcanic magnesium and calcium ions at exact PPM ratios for silky extraction balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are reservations strictly required to visit?

Walk-in patrons are always welcomed warmly at our Espresso Bar and Sunlit Veranda. Reservations are suggested for parties wishing to experience our seated Velvet Salon, Cupping Flights, or private tasting tables during peak afternoon hours.

Can I purchase whole bean roasts to brew at home?

Yes. All 14 rare single-origin roasts and signature blends are sealed in nitrogen-flushed, matte-obsidian tin canisters with one-way aroma valves. Our baristas will happily grind them to your specific brewing method (Espresso, V60, Chemex, French Press).

Do you offer plant-based milk and gluten-free pastries?

Certainly. We steam organic house-made sprouted almond milk, artisanal oat milk, and macadamia milk formulated specifically for latte art microfoam. Our pâtisserie atelier also crafts daily gluten-free almond financiers and chocolate hazelnut tortes.

Is high-speed Wi-Fi available for work?

Complimentary gigabit Wi-Fi and discreet brass power outlets are integrated into the marble desks of our Sunlit Veranda and Library Salon, making Aurelia a favored hideaway for focused creative sessions.
